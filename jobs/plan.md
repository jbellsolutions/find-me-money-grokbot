# Job: plan

The morning plan. Short: one page the other jobs read.

## Read first

- Yesterday's `daily/<yesterday>/*.md` and `log/jobs/` for yesterday.
- `repo/campaigns/find-me-money/LAUNCH.md`: the steps, in order.
- `requests/justin/`: what is waiting on him. `context/`: which switches are on.
- `state/heartbeat.jsonl`: which jobs ran.
- `context/targets.md`: the two daily numbers and the split.

## Write `daily/<today>/plan.md`

1. **Yesterday against the two numbers**, first: leads found of 100 (direct; affiliates and referral partners), conversations started of 100 (and first messages ready but not sent). Then: pieces drafted and posted, asks found and answered, partners found and contacted, contacts and opportunities in the CRM, replies. A number you cannot read is "?".
2. **The next open step of LAUNCH.md**, and which job does it today.
3. **Today, per job**, and how today's 100 and 100 are split across lead-radar, communities and partnerships: what to make, from which file, and the check that shows it is done. Content gets the most: name the three morning pieces by pillar and type.
4. **The weakest number**, and the one change today that goes after it.
5. **Waiting on Justin**: only items not already in his queue. Add each new one to `requests/justin/` once.

Do not do the other jobs' work here.
