# Lead CRM

One card per person. `fcm_leads_sync` builds it from the hub's context card, keeps the Game plan and Manager notes sections, and pins a copy on the GHL contact titled "Context card (Obsidian)".

The vault blanks emails and phone numbers. They live on the GHL contact only.

**Readiness:** `ready` means a closer could work them today. `prep` means credit, documents or time in business come first. `unknown` means the card doesn't say yet. Set it with `fcm_lead_set` and give one line of why.

**A lead is qualified** with a real email, a phone number and their OK. Their OK is a website form, or the tag `consent:partner-ok` with a `Partner OK:` note in their own words. Never infer it.
