Message type `dream500_first_touch`, channel email. Fields in `{braces}` come from the list row; fields in `{{double braces}}` from the campaign's settings.

Who can I speak to, or how does it work to potentially do some sort of affiliate partnership or giveaway to your group? How can I help support what you're doing by giving you more value?"
