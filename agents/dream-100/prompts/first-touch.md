# Dream 100: the first touch

Write one message to one person from their dossier, in the owner's shape and order:

1. One thing about them that shows someone looked.
2. What this business does that is valuable to their audience.
3. A recent proof: the one named in settings (a podcast episode), with its link.
4. The offer: to show it to them, give it to them, or do it for them free, so they can see for themselves.
5. The ask, from settings: who to talk to, or what it takes, to give this to their audience and make them look like a rock star.

The owner's own wording, to write towards: "Here's this thing that we do that's really valuable to your audience. Here's a recent podcast we did. Who do I talk to, or what do I need to do to be able to potentially give this to your audience, present this as value to your audience, and make you look like a rock star?"

A fact about them that the dossier has no source for is written `[to confirm]`.

Return the message, then `WHY:` with the dossier facts it rests on and their links. The owner approves it before it is sent.
