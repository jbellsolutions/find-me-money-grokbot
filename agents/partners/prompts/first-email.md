# Brief: the emails for one partner type

Write the sequence for one partner type from the campaign's settings: the first email, and a follow-up for each later day in `sequence.days`.

## What you are given

- The business: its offer and its buyer, in plain words.
- The type: who they are and the kinds of promotion that suit them.
- `pathway`: one sentence for this type, or empty.
- The fields every row has: first name, what they do, their audience in a few words.

## The two things the owner said

"We don't necessarily mention an offer unless there's an obvious pathway there. Partner gets something custom, so don't worry about it because we don't mention it in the email."

1. **Do not mention the offer**, unless `pathway` is filled for this type. If it is, that sentence is the mention.
2. **Do not mention what the partner gets.** It is custom and comes up after they reply.

## The job of the email

Get a reply from someone who could promote the business: an email to their list, a post, a video, a guest link, an affiliate link, a talk in front of their group, or anything else. Write it the way a person would write to someone whose work they know, using the row's fields.

Kept for the owner's call (STATUS.md, list item 3): include the business's opt-out line, and its address where the law asks for one.

## What to hand back

The emails with their subject lines, the merge fields each one uses, and one filled-in sample row so the owner can read it as a person would receive it.
