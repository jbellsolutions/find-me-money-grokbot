# Partners, collaborators and influencers

The wide channel: partners, collaborators and influencers who are not Dream 100 or 500 but could be big hits. A couple of promotions a month is the win. Cold email at volume: 1,000 a day, 10 per inbox, to anyone active who is a good potential partner and has not been identified as Dream 100 or Dream 500.

| | |
|---|---|
| Engine | grokbot |
| Tier | 3 |
| Channels | email, linkedin, x, instagram, youtube |
| Message types | partner_outreach, collab_outreach, partner_terms |

## What it is built from today

In the `revenue-os-max` kit (the product's base, in the same factory):

- Roles: `roles/partners.md`, `roles/social.md`
- Skills: `collab-blitz`, `partner-fit-review`, `partner-conversation-drafts`, `partner-attribution-review`, `affiliate-activation-support`
- Worker jobs: none
