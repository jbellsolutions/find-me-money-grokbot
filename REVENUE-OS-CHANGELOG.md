# Changelog: the Revenue OS product, by function

Every accepted change to the product gets one entry here. An application chat applies a feature by reading its entry and the files it points to, then changing only its own business. Nothing else needs to be relayed.

**Function** is one of the nine agents, or `core` for what they share (engines, the conversation log, the LinkedIn path, the gates). **Version** counts up per function. **Apply** says what an application has to do to take it.

## Adding an entry

The function's chat proposes the line with its work; the product's main chat adds it when the work is accepted into the template. One line per feature, not per commit. If a change touches two functions it gets two lines.
