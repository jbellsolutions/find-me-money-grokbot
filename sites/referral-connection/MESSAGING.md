## The position

The Referral Connection is where a business gets customers from another business instead of buying them. Most of what a local owner is sold for growth is a toll: an ad, a lead list, a directory that charges for the click. This is the other way. Two owners who share a kind of customer and don't compete tell their own people about each other, and pay each other only when a sale happens.

What it disrupts: paying for attention up front, with the risk all on the owner.

## What it stands for

1. The listing is free.
2. Owners deal with each other directly.
3. Commission is paid on a sale, not on a promise.
4. A partner is someone with the same customer and a different product.
5. Each owner's list stays their own. Nobody's contacts are handed over.
6. Every pairing shown as an example is real and shown with both owners' yes, or it is plainly made up.

Each is a promise the site has to keep before it is said in public. Line 1 is true only while the listing is free; line 3 is how the site describes a referral campaign and is not a rule it enforces.

## The eight education pillars

| # | Pillar | What the owner learns |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What a customer costs you now | How to work out what an ad, a bought lead and a referral each cost per sale, from the owner's own numbers |
| 2 | "You send to my list. I send to yours." | How a list swap works, start to finish, with one email each |
| 3 | Picking a partner | Same customer, different product, same area; who to say no to |
| 4 | Commission deals | What to pay, how to track it, how to write it down in one page |
| 5 | Sponsorships | What a sponsor gets, what to ask for, what a local sponsorship is worth |
| 6 | Collaborations and joint offers | A shared event, a bundle, a co-written guide: what each takes |
| 7 | Protecting your list and your name | What to check before you put your name beside someone else's |
| 8 | Your first partner in your area | The three businesses nearest you that already have your customer |

Each pillar is a guide on the site (the AEO and SEO agent), a set of content pieces (the content agent) and a reason to write to someone (cold email, the Dream lists).

## Content types

- **Reactions:** a real "buy our leads" or "get on page one" pitch on screen, sender covered, and what it would cost per sale.
- **Talkbacks:** an answer to a line owners hear ("you have to pay to play").
- **Breakdowns:** one pairing or one deal worked through in under a minute.
- **UGC:** owners showing a pairing that worked, with both owners' permission each time.

## Voice

- One owner talking to another. Plain words, plain numbers.
- One pairing or one deal per piece.
- Respectful of the owner, hard on the toll.

It never: promises customers, sales or income; quotes a cost for ads or leads without a source the reader can open; names a company as a wrongdoer without a court finding or a regulator's action to point at; shows a business, a person or a message without their yes.
