# Campaign rules

For anyone on the intent list or any other cold list.

## What the list is

Research-intent lists are never treated as opt-in: a research signal is not an application or consent to contact. That
makes this cold B2B email under CAN-SPAM. It's legal with an honest From and subject, a real postal address and a working
opt-out. It must also be clear the email is a solicitation.

## The lines

- Email only. Never text or call these numbers: there's no TCPA consent.
- Never say we saw them searching. Never imply a past relationship or an inquiry they didn't make.
- Never promise approval, a rate, terms or a timeline. "May qualify" until a partner has reviewed them.
- Never ask for documents, bank logins, SSN, date of birth or a credit report by email.
- We're a broker. We never lend, and we never say we do.
- 0% means "0% credit lines". Never "business credit cards".
- Brand is Find Me Money Group. Never name the legal entity, the broker of record or the platform behind an application.
- Someone who says stop, in any words, hears nothing more from any tool. A complaint goes to Justin.
- Asked if this is AI or if it's really Justin: don't answer. It goes to Justin.

## Sorting a reply

| Reply | What happens |
|---|---|
| Stop, unsubscribe, no thanks, no, nope, pass, not for me | Suppressed everywhere, no draft, no contact added |
| A complaint | Suppressed, flagged for Justin |
| Bounce | Suppressed |
| Out of office | Logged. The sender keeps its own schedule |
| Wrong person | Not a Fit, suppressed. Don't treat a referral as permission |
| Not interested | Not a Fit, suppressed |
| Not now | Stays open, tagged, one draft offering to check back |
| Interested, with something to go on | They Answered, the funding map as a held draft. A person sends it the same day |
| Interested, nothing to go on yet | They Answered, a held draft asking what it's for and roughly how much |
| A question | They Answered, a task. Answer what they asked first, then ask one thing |
| Where did you get my email | The source draft. If they press, it goes to Justin |
| Is this a bot, is this Justin, who is this | Goes to Justin, no draft |
| "send it" | Added to the monthly note. Nobody gets added any other way |
| Anything unclear | Goes to a person |

## What a partner needs

What it's for, how much, how soon, their email, their number, and their OK for a partner in their own words. Nothing else.
Partners do the credit pull and the application.
